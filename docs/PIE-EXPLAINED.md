You're writing a quote for a 12-person dinner on Saturday. Filet mignon, wild salmon, organic asparagus, truffle oil.

**What does it actually cost?**

Sometimes it does. Sometimes you eat the difference.

**Every chef in America does this.** Private chefs, caterers, restaurant owners, food truck operators, meal prep companies. Billions of dollars in food purchasing decisions made on memory, guesswork, and gut feeling.

## What Makes PIE Different

### It's not a price list. It's intelligence.

A price list tells you chicken costs $3.49/lb. PIE tells you:

- Chicken costs **$3.49/lb** at Stop & Shop in your zip code
- That's **12% cheaper** than the regional average
- It was **$2.99/lb** two weeks ago (trending up)
- Confidence: **high** (based on 4 local sources)
- If that's too much, turkey breast is **$2.79/lb** right now

### It adjusts to where you are.

The same gallon of milk costs different amounts in every state. PIE knows. A chef in Mississippi sees Mississippi prices. A chef in Hawaii sees Hawaii prices. Not a national average that's wrong for everyone.

### It never shows you a blank.

Most systems show "no data available" and leave you stuck. PIE has 13 layers of fallback. If it can't find a local store price, it checks regional data. Then state data. Then government data. Then it estimates using everything it knows about that type of food in your area. Every ingredient always gets a price, and every price tells you how confident it is.

### It runs itself.

PIE collects prices 24/7 from thousands of sources automatically. No chef ever enters data. No one maintains it. It just works, getting smarter every day as it learns seasonal patterns and improves its estimates.

### It costs nothing to run.

PIE runs on a small computer the size of a deck of cards. No cloud subscriptions. No monthly API bills. No per-query fees. The cost of serving one more chef is basically zero.

## How Accurate Is It?

Every price PIE shows you comes with a confidence score:

- **High confidence** (0.8-1.0): Based on real store data from your area, within the last few days. Trust it.
- **Medium confidence** (0.5-0.8): Based on regional averages or slightly older data. Reliable ballpark.
- **Low confidence** (below 0.5): Estimated from government data, category averages, or neighboring regions. Use as a starting point, verify at the store.

PIE never hides this. You always know exactly how much to trust any number you see.

**Target accuracy: within 15% of actual shelf price, 90% of the time.** That's the bar. PIE measures itself against it continuously and improves every month.
