# Contextual Rail: Intelligence Category Research

## Why This Matters

The Rail isn't a status bar. It's the operational nervous system. To design the right intelligence categories, we study what makes the world's most successful businesses excellent — then map those principles to what a chef needs to see on every page.

| #   | Category          | Icon         | What It Answers                                                    |
| --- | ----------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | **Readiness**     | Progress bar | How complete/ready is this thing? What's missing?                  |
| 2   | **Money**         | Dollar       | What's the financial picture? Costs, margins, payments, forecasts? |

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES (from David's direction)

1. **Collapsible** — completely out of your face when collapsed. One-line summary with key stats.
2. **Hover for details** — hover over any intel card and see more without clicking away. Tooltips, popovers, expanded views.
3. **Inline actions** — check things off, send messages, approve items directly from the Rail. No page navigation required.
4. **Quick menus** — some items open a mini-panel/dropdown right there. Mark complete, snooze, delegate.
5. **Not a status bar** — it's a context-aware operational copilot. It THINKS about what you're doing.
6. **Main artery + bloodlines** — Dashboard Rail = main artery showing everything. Per-URL Rail = bloodline specifically for that page, fed by every engine that touches it.
7. **URL-aware** — every URL eventually knows how it connects to every other URL. Full cohesiveness.
8. **Temporal** — knows what you were likely doing before, what you'll do next, what changed recently.

## THE SKILL: `/rail-audit`

A skill that goes URL by URL and optimizes the Rail for each page:

- What does this page do?
- What is the user doing on this page?
- What was the user most likely doing before this page?
- What is the user most likely doing after this page?
- Who/what is wired to this page?
- Which of the 8 intelligence categories apply?
- What engines/resolvers feed this page?
- What does the Rail physically look like on this page?
- What can the user act on directly from the Rail here?
- What should NOT appear in the Rail on this page?

This skill runs iteratively, page by page, building Rail Profiles that the system uses to render context-aware intelligence.
