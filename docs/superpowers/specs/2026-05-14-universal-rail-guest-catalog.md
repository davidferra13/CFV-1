# Universal Rail: Guest Role - Complete Item Catalog

> Date: 2026-05-14
> Role: `guest` - signed in, persistent account, no committed chef/client role yet.
> Scope: Every item type that can appear on the Universal Rail for guests.
> Rule: Guest rail inherits public discovery, adds memory and conversion nudges, and excludes client/chef operational signals.

## Positioning

Guests are the conversion layer between anonymous discovery and committed ChefFlow usage. The rail should feel like ChefFlow remembers what they like and is making discovery easier without forcing a booking or chef profile decision.

Guest rail jobs:

1. Improve discovery through saved chefs, saved locations, recent searches, comparison state, browse history, and preference-weighted scoring.
2. Gently convert toward client or chef role when behavior indicates intent.
3. Never show operational client or chef work: no event lifecycle, quote lifecycle, submitted inquiry lifecycle, payments, messages, task work, staff work, or admin diagnostics.

## Existing Contracts Used

Guest actions come from `ROLE_ACTIONS.guest` in `lib/discovery/rail-contract-registry.ts`: `navigate`, `select`, `filter`, `save`, `hide`, `lock`, `compare`, and `share`.

Guest-capable feature flags in `lib/discovery/rail-feature-flags.ts`: `recent_searches`, `saved_locations`, `compare_mode`, `shortlist_drawer`, `discovery_session`, `universal_filter_state`, `active_filter_summary`, `clear_all_reset`, `smart_empty_results_repair`, and `remy_tuning` when Remy is available.

Core persistence and personalization sources:

- `consumer_saved_chefs`
- `discovery_interactions`
- `discovery_profile_items`
- `lib/discovery/saved-chefs.ts`
- `lib/discovery/consumer-discovery-model.ts`
- `lib/discovery/preference-contract.ts`
- `lib/discovery/personalization-scoring.ts`
- Public routes under `app/(public)/`

## Legend

| Field               | Meaning                                                                                                                                      |

| `baseUrgency`       | Static 0-100 priority before personalization, page affinity, recency, and diversity modifiers.                                               |
| `urgencyDecayFn`    | `deadline`, `linear`, or `none`. Deadline items peak near the event/window and expire. Linear items fade with age. None items are evergreen. |

| `privacy level`     | `public`, `account_private`, `behavior_private`, `preference_private`, or `aggregate_public`.                                                |

## Guest Privacy Rules

- Behavioral memory is private to the signed-in guest account.
- Preference inference can explain itself, but should use soft language: "seems like", "based on recent activity", "you have saved".
- Social proof must use aggregate thresholds and never reveal specific guest identities.
- Saved chef and recent-search items can reference guest actions but must not expose private IDs in URLs.
- An abandoned inquiry item is allowed only for local or account draft state that was not submitted. Once submitted, it becomes client operational work and is not part of the guest rail.

- Cuisines: `cuisine`, `trending_cuisine_nearby`.

### Guest Persistence Items

Persistence is the reason the guest rail should feel meaningfully better than public.

- `saved_chef_resurface` should be the default saved-chef memory item. It appears when a saved chef has no stronger update or availability signal.
- `saved` is the generic persistence item for saved cuisines, dishes, filters, collections, and other discovery objects. It should yield to more specific saved-chef and saved-location items.
- `saved_chef_update` outranks `saved_chef_resurface` for the same chef and should be shown only when the update is genuinely new since save or last profile view.
- `saved_chef_availability` is the strongest saved-chef item because it combines memory with timely action.
- `comparison_history` appears after a guest compared at least two chefs. It should restore the exact candidates if still public and valid.
- `recent_search` is the lightweight persistence item. It should be clickable, editable, and clearable.
- `browse_pattern` and `preference_evolution` are explainable learning items. `browse_pattern` says what ChefFlow noticed; `preference_evolution` asks whether to save it as a durable profile signal.
- `abandoned_inquiry_draft` is allowed only before submission. Submitted inquiries move the user into client-role operational territory and must not be shown in the guest catalog.

### Conversion Nudges

Guest nudges must be earned by behavior. No conversion nudge should be first-screen noise for a brand-new guest.

- `first_booking_nudge`: after 3+ chef views, 2+ saves, compare mode usage, or an inquiry draft.
- `taste_profile_completion`: after 5+ meaningful interactions or any profile step started.
- `dietary_setup_nudge`: after repeated dietary filtering, never from a single accidental click.
- `location_save_nudge`: after 2+ location searches or strong local dwell.
- `chef_facing_content_nudge`: only after chef/operator-facing route behavior.
- `dinner_circle_nudge`: after group-size or occasion behavior.
- `personalized_recs_nudge`: after 10+ interactions without usable preferences.
- `profile_progress`: after profile work begins, not before.

Only one conversion nudge should appear in the first visible rail segment. If multiple qualify, use:

1. Safety/profile completion nudges (`dietary_setup_nudge`, `profile_progress`).
2. Unfinished action recovery (`abandoned_inquiry_draft`).
3. Clear role-intent nudge (`first_booking_nudge` or `chef_facing_content_nudge`).
4. Softer preference or circle nudges.

### Seasonal And Timely Items

Timely items should feel helpful, not alarmist.

- `holiday_plan` starts early enough to be useful and peaks before inventory gets tight.
- `weekend_availability` starts on Monday, gets strongest Wednesday-Friday, and expires Sunday night.
- `local_event` requires a saved or searched location.
- `seasonal_ingredient` and `seasonal` should show real seasonal rationale and never fake local freshness.

### Social Proof

Social proof uses only aggregate, thresholded data.

- Never render `social_booking_count`, `most_saved_chef`, or `trending_cuisine_nearby` below privacy thresholds.
- Prefer relative proof over exact numbers when counts are small.
- Do not stack more than two social proof items in one rail pass.
- Social proof can support a chef or cuisine but should not replace direct preference match reasons.

### Cross-Sell

Cross-sell items are intentionally low urgency.

- `cross_sell_gift_card` gets seasonal boosts and chef-profile boosts.
- `cross_sell_referral` should appear only after the guest has had enough value to plausibly share ChefFlow.
- Cross-sell never outranks saved-chef availability, draft recovery, empty-result recovery, or strong preference items.

## Rail Assembly Rules For Guests

### Minimum Mix

For a standard guest rail pass, target:

- 35-45 percent personalized discovery.
- 20-30 percent persistence/resume items when available.
- 10-20 percent timely/social proof.
- 5-15 percent conversion nudges.
- 0-10 percent cross-sell.

For brand-new guests with little data, fall back to public discovery plus `personalized_recs_nudge` only after enough interactions.

### Suppression And Diversity

- Do not show two cards for the same chef unless one is an expanded sub-item.
- Do not show more than one role-choice nudge at once.
- Do not show more than two social proof items at once.
- Do not show cross-sell in the first three slots unless the current route is `/gift-cards`.
- Do not show `first_booking_nudge` before the guest has shown chef booking intent.
- Do not show `chef_facing_content_nudge` from normal food discovery alone.
- Always honor `hide`, `dismiss`, `hate`, `never_show`, and hard dietary exclusions.

### Priority Arbitration

Use this order when scores tie:

1. Route-context recovery: `recovery_empty_results`, `recovery_not_found`.
2. Unfinished guest action: `abandoned_inquiry_draft`.
3. Timely saved item: `saved_chef_availability`, `weekend_availability`.
4. Fresh saved item: `saved_chef_update`, `saved_chef_resurface`.
5. Explicit profile or safety prompt: `dietary_setup_nudge`, `profile_progress`.
6. Preference-matched discovery: `cuisine`, `food_type`, `featured_chef`, `ingredient`.
7. Recent memory: `recent_search`, `comparison_history`, `browse_pattern`.
8. Aggregate/timely discovery: `trending`, `local_event`, `holiday_plan`, `social_booking_count`.
9. Editorial delight: `chef_pick`, `editorial_collection`, `story`, `surprise`.
10. Soft conversion and cross-sell: `first_booking_nudge`, `dinner_circle_nudge`, `cross_sell_gift_card`, `cross_sell_referral`.

## Implementation Notes

### Type Contract

Current production `DiscoveryItemType` includes 24 base public types. Guest-only items in this catalog are product types that can be implemented as:

1. New union members when the Universal Rail becomes role-aware.
2. `saved`, `combo`, `story`, `chef_pick`, or `featured_chef` wrappers with `guestSubtype` metadata during migration.

Do not leak private identifiers in hrefs. Follow `discoveryHrefHasPrivateIdentifier` constraints: no raw `userId`, `tenantId`, `chefId`, `sourceId`, `sessionId`, or UUID query values.

### Analytics

Guest item analytics should preserve the existing event grammar:

- Impression: `discovery_rail_impression`
- Click: `discovery_rail_click`
- Select/filter: `discovery_filter_apply`
- Save: `discovery_item_save`
- Hide/dismiss: `discovery_item_hide`
- Lock: `discovery_item_lock`
- Compare: `discovery_item_compare`
- Share: `discovery_item_share`

Guest-specific metadata should include `guest_subtype`, `source_signal`, `privacy_level`, `algorithm_version`, and route/page path. Metadata must be bounded and should not contain private IDs.

### Expiration Defaults

If an item omits explicit data:

- Saved chef memory: 45 days from last guest action.
- Recent search: 30 days.
- Compare history: 30 days.
- Preference inference: 30 days or until reviewed.
- Editorial: campaign end or 30 days.
- Seasonal: season end.
- Social proof: daily or weekly recompute.
- Conversion nudges: until completed, dismissed, or role conversion.

### Guest Completion Definition

The guest rail has done its job when one of these happens:

- Guest submits a booking/inquiry and becomes a client-context user.
- Guest starts/claims a chef profile and becomes chef/partner-context user.
- Guest builds enough saved/preferences state that discovery is meaningfully personalized even before conversion.

Until then, every item should either delight with better discovery or inch the guest toward a next committed role.
