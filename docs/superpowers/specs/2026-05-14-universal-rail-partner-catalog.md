# Universal Rail: Partner Role - Complete Item Catalog

> **Date:** 2026-05-14
> **Role:** `partner` (external collaborator: referral partner, venue owner, co-host, event planner, vendor, supplier)
> **Scope:** Every item type that can appear on the Universal Rail for partner users.
> **Routes:** `app/(partner)/partner/` with current primary pages `/partner/dashboard`, `/partner/locations`, `/partner/locations/[id]`, `/partner/events`, `/partner/profile`, `/partner/preview`.
> **Design philosophy:** The partner rail is a business relationship dashboard. It answers: "How is my partnership with ChefFlow performing, what opportunities are nearby, and what needs my attention?"

## Legend

| **privacy**           | Partner may see only their own referrals, partner locations, co-hosted events, routed inquiries, assets, payouts, and compliance records. Never show other partners' data or chef-only operations. |

### Privacy Contract

Every partner rail resolver must start from `requirePartner()` and scope data by one of:

- `referral_partners.id = user.partnerId`
- `partner_locations.partner_id = user.partnerId`
- `partner_images.partner_id = user.partnerId`
- `events.referral_partner_id = user.partnerId`
- `events.partner_location_id IN (partner's location IDs)`
- co-host/collaboration membership where the partner is explicitly granted access
- payout, campaign, message, task, and compliance tables keyed to `partner_id`

Partners may see event-safe fields: date, time window, location, status, guest count, ticket counts, task status, shared dietary constraints, shared planning thread excerpts, and first names only where the relationship created the referral or co-host access. Partners do not see chef financial operations, private client profiles, internal notes, other partners, or unrelated venue data.

### Suppression and Grouping Rules

- Group by `eventId` first. For the same event, show the highest urgency parent plus at most three child items unless the partner expands the group.
- `cohost_event_readiness` becomes the parent when readiness is below 80% or any partner-owned task is overdue.
- `weather_alert_for_venue_event`, `guest_dietary_needs_for_venue`, and compliance blockers override normal event progress because they are safety/risk items.
- `referral_converted` suppresses `referral_pending` for the same referral.
- `referral_payout_ready` suppresses individual earning items once the balance can be paid.
- `payout_method_setup_needed` and `tax_document_needed` outrank payout-ready and payout-processed items when they block money movement.
- `partner_next_best_action` may summarize one lower-priority item but must link back to the underlying source item for auditability.
- Marketing items must be suppressed during unresolved compliance, security, payout-blocker, or event-safety items.
- Onboarding parent item suppresses onboarding children only while expanded; otherwise the highest urgency incomplete child can appear independently.
- Vendor/supplier items are visible only for supplier/vendor partner types or explicit event assignment.
- Event planner items are visible only for planner partner types or explicit collaboration grants.

### Implementation Guardrails

- The rail resolver for partner role must never reuse chef rail queries without an explicit partner-safe projection.
- Every item should carry `sourceEntityType`, `sourceEntityId`, `partnerId`, optional `eventId`, optional `locationId`, and `privacyLevel = partner_scope`.
- Partner-facing event links should land inside `/partner/events` or `/partner/locations`, not chef workspace routes.
- If a source is not yet implemented, the contract can exist behind a feature flag with a resolver returning zero items.
- Hover previews must be redacted the same way as click destinations. A hover preview is not a weaker privacy boundary.
- Public showcase data can be linked, but the rail item itself still resolves from the authenticated partner scope.
