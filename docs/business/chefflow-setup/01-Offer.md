# ChefFlow Setup

## Online pickup, set up and handed over

We help independent restaurants get direct online pickup working on an established ordering platform. We configure the agreed setup, check that an order reaches your team, and show you how to run it.

### Pilot: $500 once

One restaurant location. Up to 30 standard menu items with simple options. The first three accepted projects.

Included:

- A review of your current website, order intake and point-of-sale setup.
- Selection of a suitable provider with your approval of its costs.
- Configuration of the agreed menu, pickup hours, preparation times and order notification route.
- An ordering link on your existing website when the required access is available.
- An agreed test order and a staff walkthrough.
- Written instructions and 14 days of fixes for defects in the agreed setup.

You own the accounts, billing and recovery details. There is no required ongoing ChefFlow Setup subscription.

Payment: $250 after scope approval and $250 after acceptance and handover. Provider subscriptions, processing fees, hardware and applicable taxes are separate. We identify costs before you commit.

Timing is agreed after the menu, account access and any provider verification are ready.

### A clear scope

Full website redesign, point-of-sale migration, complex modifiers, more than 30 items, custom integrations, delivery logistics, custom catering checkout and ongoing menu management need a separate scope.

This offer does not guarantee additional orders, revenue, platform approval or a provider referral payment. If we may receive a referral fee from a recommended provider, we disclose it before you choose.

### The first conversation

We confirm whether online pickup would help, which system you already use, and how staff should receive and fulfill orders. If your existing tools already solve the problem, we tell you.
