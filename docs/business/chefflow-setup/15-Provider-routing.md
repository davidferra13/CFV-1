# Provider routing for the first pickup setup

Internal decision aid, reviewed 27 September 2026. This is not a provider endorsement, merchant agreement, application, or instruction to contact anyone. Verify each merchant's present stack and written quote before selecting a service.

## Decide from the merchant's current operation

1. First inspect the system already taking in-person orders. If it has a usable direct pickup feature, configure that path when the merchant wants it and approves its full cost. Avoid creating a second menu and order queue merely to pursue a referral.
2. Confirm whether staff can receive, acknowledge and fulfill test orders without monitoring a new device. Check pickup hours, sold-out items, modifiers, tips, taxes, refunds and payment destination with the merchant.
3. Compare the total first-year merchant cost, including subscription, processing, per-order charges, equipment, and cancellation terms. Obtain the live account quote; public list prices are only a screening input.
4. Ask who owns the account, billing, recovery, menu and customer data. Record the agreed handover and any referral compensation disclosure before a provider decision.
5. If the chosen provider requires a Marketplace listing or POS migration that the merchant does not want, stop and rescope. The $500 pilot covers one direct pickup setup, not migration or new delivery operations.

## Keep three kinds of DoorDash orders separate

DoorDash's public US pricing says Marketplace delivery carries 15%, 25% or 30% commission by plan, and Marketplace pickup is 6%. Orders placed through DoorDash Online Ordering on the restaurant's own channels are described as commission-free, with a processing fee. The direct-order product requires an active DoorDash store. Do not call the whole relationship "free pickup" or imply that joining Marketplace is part of the $500 direct-pickup service.

## Referral reality

Square's public referral-partner page requires an application, an agreement and its tracked link; its advertised $1,000+ applies to qualifying referrals processing $250,000+ annually. No acceptance, tracking link or eligible merchant has been verified here.

DoorDash's merchant-referral page requires the referrer to be a current US Marketplace partner in good standing. It caps bonuses at ten merchants, requires permission from the referred owner, and requires 15 orders within 60 days; payment can follow within another 60 days after qualification. This is not presently a ChefFlow agency commission. No referral or application is authorized.

For planning, service revenue remains the proposed $500 setup fee per accepted job and referral revenue remains $0. The eight-hour, $50/hour internal cost assumption leaves $100 before overhead and taxes if actual hours and direct costs match assumptions. No projects or referral payments have been recorded.
