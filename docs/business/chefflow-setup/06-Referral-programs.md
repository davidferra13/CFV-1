## Recommendation

Start with the installation service as the proposed revenue source. Treat referral compensation as $0 in the base case until approved, qualified and paid. Select the provider for the owner's existing systems, fulfillment and total cost; a commission is secondary.

Before recommending: confirm applicable monthly fees, processing, per-order fees, hardware, cancellation, supported locations and whether integration with the current point-of-sale is actually supported. Do not quote pricing from memory.

Use GitHub only when a real software repository or custom development need exists. A restaurant needing ordering generally needs an ordering provider and staff workflow, not a GitHub account.

## Square application description — not submitted

ChefFlow Setup is an early-stage implementation service for independent restaurants in the Merrimack Valley and nearby New Hampshire. We are preparing a three-merchant pilot focused on direct online pickup, owner-controlled accounts, verified order delivery and a documented handover.

We are interested in the referral partner program for merchants whose needs and processing volume fit Square. We have not completed paid merchant implementations under this service and do not claim certified partner status. Please confirm program eligibility, qualifying activity, referral attribution, payment timing, and whether implementation support may accompany a referral.

The legal applicant, operating history, website, merchant count and expected processing volume will be supplied accurately. No unverified numbers will be submitted as facts.

## Commission records

Keep provider/application status, agreement date, eligible merchant criteria, consent to refer, attribution link/ID, activation/activity evidence, expected payout date, received date and amount. Pending, earned and received are different states. Disclose compensation to the merchant before provider selection.
