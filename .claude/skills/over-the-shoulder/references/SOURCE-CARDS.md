# Source Cards

These are pre-researched warm-start cards. They summarize public, official, or well-known source material checked on 2026-05-15. Browse again for latest/current details, pricing, product availability, policy, or awards.

## Take a Chef

Use for: private chef marketplace, in-home dining, chef-client matching.

Source basis:

- Official site: https://www.takeachef.com/
- US private chef page: https://www.takeachef.com/en-us/

Public pattern:

- Position the service around private chefs cooking in the customer's home.
- Treat the dining table/home context as the emotional center of the experience.
- Make chef discovery, menu expectation, location, date, and event context visible early.

ChefFlow mimic:

- Design booking as a trust-building match, not a generic lead form.
- Make menu, chef profile, allergies, guest count, service style, and price expectation visible together.
- Show the client exactly what happens next after inquiry or proposal.

Avoid:

- Reducing private chef work to restaurant reservation UX.
