# Over The Shoulder Examples

## Chef Booking Flow

Selected lenses:

- Take a Chef - direct private chef marketplace comparison.
- HoneyBook - proposal, contract, invoice, and approval flow.
- Tock - reservations, deposits, and timed premium experiences.
- Airbnb - trust, profile, review, and hospitality presentation.
- Stripe - payment states, deposits, refunds, and receipts.

Skipped:

- NASA - too safety-critical for this pass unless booking failure has severe downstream risk.
- YouTube - ranking is not the main problem unless discovery/feed quality is in scope.

## Chef Dashboard

Selected lenses:

- Toast - food-service operations and day-of-service workflows.
- QuickBooks - finance/accounting vocabulary and admin clarity.
- Google Workspace - files, calendar, docs, and sharing.
- Linear - dense workflow UX that stays fast and scannable.

## Pricing And Tax

Selected lenses:
