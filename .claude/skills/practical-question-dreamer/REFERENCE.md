# Practical Question Dreamer Reference

Use this reference when the question pass needs sharper prompts from analysts, leaders, and elite Silicon Valley-style engineers.

## Amazing Analyst Questions

- What is the base rate?
- What changed, and what stayed constant?
- What is the denominator?
- Is this signal or sampling bias?
- What would we expect to see if the opposite were true?
- Which segment is hiding the real story?
- What is the leading indicator, not the lagging one?
- What decision becomes different if this analysis is true?

Analyst questions should expose mechanics, measurement quality, segmentation, and whether the evidence actually changes a decision.

## Strong Leader Questions

- What matters most right now?
- Who owns the outcome?
- What are we pretending not to know?
- What tradeoff are we avoiding naming?
- Where are incentives misaligned?
- What needs to be said plainly?
- What decision are we delaying by asking for more information?
- What would make this team faster, clearer, or more accountable next week?

Leader questions should expose priority, ownership, incentives, avoided tradeoffs, accountability, and the cost of delay.

## Elite Engineer Questions

- What is the simplest thing that could work?
- Where does this fail at scale?
- What invariant must always hold?
- What is the real bottleneck?
- What happens during retries, partial failure, bad input, or stale state?
- Can this be deleted instead of improved?
- What abstraction are we prematurely inventing?
- How would we prove this works in production, not just locally?

Engineer questions should expose invariants, failure modes, deletion opportunities, over-abstraction, bottlenecks, and production proof.

## Shared Pattern

The strongest questions keep returning to:

- What is actually true?
- What is actually useful?
- What breaks?
- What decision does this unlock?
- What is the smallest proof?
- What are we not seeing because the current system hides it?

Do not use these questions as a generic list. Translate them into the current product surface, workflow, persona, data source, or queue decision.
