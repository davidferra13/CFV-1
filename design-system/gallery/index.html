Colour

Every colour comes from a role. The brand ramp is swapped at runtime across eight palettes, which is why a hardcoded hex is a correctness bug and not a style preference.

Brand ramp

Stops 600 and 700 are deliberately identical: the light-mode focus ring resolves to brand-600 and measures 4.63:1 there.

Neutral ramp

Inverted in light mode on purpose, so dark-first utility usage renders correctly on a light ground. zinc, gray and neutral are aliases of this scale.

Motion

Motion explains hierarchy, state, causality or spatial movement. Nothing animates to look richer. Hover a bar to see its duration.

duration-instant75ms
duration-fast100ms
duration-normal200ms
duration-slow350ms
duration-enter220ms
duration-exit150ms
duration-deliberate500ms

Under prefers-reduced-motion everything here collapses to 0.01ms and entrance animations resolve to their final state. Spinners, skeleton shimmer and progress fills are exempt, because they are the only feedback that work is happening.

Overlays

Dialogs trap focus, close on Escape, restore focus, and carry the full ARIA. Destructive and confirm actions sit apart.

Delete this event?

This cannot be undone. Destructive actions are never adjacent to confirm actions.

Media

Real photography, kept legible. Do not bury important media under an aggressive gradient.

Text over photography

Only .media-scrim. Never a flat colour wash, and never below 4.5:1.
